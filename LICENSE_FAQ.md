# DeepNight SDK License FAQ ⚖️

This document clarifies how you can use DeepNight SDK in your projects.

## 1. Is there a free version?
Yes. The SDK is distributed under the **Apache 2.0 License** for community use. You can use the public modules (Kotlin/Compose) and the simplified native binaries in your open-source or personal non-commercial projects for free.

## 2. When do I need an Enterprise License?
You must contact us for an Enterprise License if:
- You are a commercial company with a revenue over $100k/year.
- You are integrating the SDK into a commercial TV Box firmware (OEM).
- You require access to the **Full C++ Source Code**.
- You need **Priority Support** and custom hardware-specific optimizations.

## 3. What is included in the Enterprise License?
- **Closed-Source Native Core**: High-performance binaries (.so) optimized for specific chipsets (Amlogic, Rockchip, etc.).
- **Indemnification**: Full legal rights to use the proprietary algorithms in your commercial products.
- **Direct Support**: 24/7 technical assistance for integration.
- **Custom Development**: We can adapt the SDK to your unique hardware or UI requirements.

## 4. How much does it cost?
We use a project-based pricing model. Since every integration is unique, we offer:
- **Per Project License**: A one-time fee for a single app/service.
- **White Label License**: Total branding freedom.
- **Royalty-based (OEM)**: Small fee per sold device.
